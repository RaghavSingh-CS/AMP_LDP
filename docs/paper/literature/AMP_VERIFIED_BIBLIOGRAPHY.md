# AMP-LDP verified journal bibliography

## Scope and admission rule

This bibliography is the Stage 29 literature set for the provisional report **“Weakly Supervised Latent Distribution Profiling for Customer-Level Electricity Theft Detection.”** An item is admitted only when its title, author list, year, journal, and DOI resolve consistently through Crossref and the journal/publisher landing page, and the journal’s Scopus or Web of Science coverage can be substantiated. Conference papers, books, chapters, preprints, and web commentary are excluded.

The set contains **24 journal articles**, of which **15 were published from 2023 onward**. Article landing pages are represented by DOI URLs so that the citation remains publisher-neutral and durable. Journal-index evidence and article-level verification notes are recorded in `AMP_REFERENCE_VERIFICATION.md`.

## Use boundary

The bibliography verifies publication identity and journal eligibility; it does not make heterogeneous experiments directly comparable. In particular, identical dataset names do not establish identical preprocessing, split membership, prevalence, balancing, label granularity, model selection, or evaluation units. Quantitative claims must be carried with their source protocol.
